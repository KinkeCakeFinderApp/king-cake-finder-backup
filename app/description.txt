FULL DESCRIPTION  (max 4000 characters)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
King Cake Finder helps you discover the best king cakes for Mardi Gras and
Carnival season — from neighborhood home bakers to established
brick-and-mortar bakeries. Browse listings, compare prices, see what's
nearby, and read honest reviews from other king cake lovers before you buy.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Browse bakeries by name, city, or flavor, and filter by what matters to
  you — home bakery vs. brick-and-mortar, shipping available, and more.
• See every bakery on an interactive map, with your distance and estimated
  travel time to each one.
• Read pricing and variations for each bakery's king cakes before you go.
• Rate and review bakeries with a simple king-cake rating — one honest
  review per bakery, per person.
• Save your favorite bakeries so they're a tap away next time.
• Keep a "To Be Tasted" wishlist of bakeries you want to try next — separate
  from your favorites, so you always know where to go next.
• Get directions in one tap via Apple Maps or Google Maps, or call a bakery
  directly from its listing.
• Browse as a guest with no account required — sign up only when you want
  to save favorites, leave a review, or message support.

SAFETY, FIRST
Every bakery page includes a food-safety reminder before you order —
covering the traditional hidden baby/trinket choking hazard, potential
allergens, and a reminder that listings are user- and owner-submitted, not
independently verified. Reviews are auto-moderated for inappropriate
content, and you can report a review or block a user at any time. Our team
reviews reports and can remove content that violates our guidelines.

YOUR ACCOUNT, YOUR CONTROL
Create a free account to save favorites, build your to-be-tasted list, and
leave reviews — or skip it and just browse. You can request account
deletion at any time right from the app (Settings → Account → Delete
account), which removes your reviews, favorites, and profile data.

King Cake Finder shares the same account and bakery data as our companion
website, so your favorites and reviews stay in sync wherever you use it.

Whether you're a lifelong New Orleanian or new to the Mardi Gras tradition,
King Cake Finder makes it easy to find your next king cake — and remember
the ones you loved.

CONTAINS ADS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Why: the app shows sponsored placements (a "Sponsored by" banner plus up to
5 admin-marked sponsored bakery listings surfaced on Home). Google Play
counts sponsored/promotional content as ads even though there is no ad SDK
and no ad tracking in the app.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
DATA SAFETY FORM — quick reference
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data collected: email address, name, user-generated reviews, location
(optional, for distance sorting), user ID.
Encryption: data is encrypted in transit (standard HTTPS/TLS via Firebase).
Data deletion: users can request account deletion in-app (Settings →
  Account → Delete account) or on the website (Account page).
Data sharing: not sold or shared with third parties.
